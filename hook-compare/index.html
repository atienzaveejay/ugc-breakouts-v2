Hook: A vs B

Same prototype, two hook flows side by side. Type a product (or tap one), answer the questions, then write the script. On the script page, tap the pencil on the hook. Sample copy.

A. Live as is

  1. Questions, then Next
  2. Choose a Hook: pick 1 of 3, Show 3 new hooks
  3. Write my script
  4. Script opens with that hook
  5. Pencil on the hook: 2 options (Question, Storytime)

B. More options in Change the hook

  1. Questions, then Write my script
  2. No hook step
  3. Script opens with 1 hook
  4. Pencil on the hook: 4 options (Question, Show it, Bold claim, Storytime)
  5. Show new hooks to refresh them (3 of 3)
