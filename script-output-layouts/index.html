Option 2 · 1 main + 2 custom

The main layout covers 7 of the 10 filming styles (talking to camera, voiceover + b-roll, GRWM, green screen, car talk, day in my life, hands only). Text on screen and skit get their own page (street interviews use the skit page). 60s+ is grouped into parts.

Which layout each format falls under
How you film itLayoutWhat changes
Talking to cameraMainspoken lines lead, short shot note under each
Voiceover + b-rollMainspoken line, the clip to show under it
Get ready with meMainone beat per routine step
Green screenMainshot note = what is on the screen behind you
Car talkMainone shot, so shot notes only on the hook
Day in my lifeMainone beat per scene, by time of day
Hands onlyMainwhat to film first, voiceover optional
Text on screenText on screenslides, or one wall of text; no spoken lines
SkitSkitcast, scenes, who says what, actions
Street interviewSkityou and the people you ask are the cast; questions are the lines, the reveal is the last scene

Inside Main, “what should the video do” names the body beats:

Sell itproblem / show it / proof
Review itfirst take / good / not so good / verdict
How to use itstep 1, 2, 3
Before and afterbefore / the switch / after
Unboxingin the box / first look / first try
Compare itA / B / verdict

Length sets how many beats: 15s = 2, 30 to 45s = 3 to 4, 60s+ = grouped into parts.
