Script output: 1 main layout and 3 for special formats

The 10 filming styles from the question screen don't need 10 layouts. 7 of them are someone saying lines over shots, so they share one main layout. Text on screen, Skit and Street interview each need a different page. In all 4, what she picked and the idea sit in their own section at the top, and a clear line shows where the script starts.

The default for 7 of the 10 filming styles. Hook, body, CTA, each line with what to film under it.

The body is not fixed. Length sets how many beats there are: 15s gets 2, 60s gets about 7 grouped into parts with a jump bar. The goal (Sell it, Compare it, How to use it, and so on) sets what the beats are. Hands only flips it so what to film comes first.
