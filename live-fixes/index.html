25

Breakout Score: the formula and its copy still use follower count

BUG + COPYStill liveFix now

Still live 19 Oct on the sales page (“views relative to follower count, engagement, and how recently the video was posted”). 11 Oct: the info text still reads “views relative to follower count, engagement, and how recently the video was posted”, and the numbers follow it. A mousepad video at 730 times its creator's usual views scores 62, while a dog video at 260 times scores 91, because the score leans on followers.

How to reproduce

Compare any result's score with the creator's usual views on their TikTok profile. Test cases: @tulip6942 (desk essentials) 730x, score 62; @winston.cavapoo (dog car seat) 260x, score 91; @kenyewest (costco finds) 3,700x, score 79.

What to build

Score the video against its own account, as agreed: usual views = median views of the creator's 15 latest non-pinned posts, leaving this one out. Then a 0 to 100 scale, for example 25 × log10(views ÷ usual views), capped at 100, so 10x = 25, 100x = 50, 1,000x = 75 and 10,000x = 100. On the test cases above that gives 72, 60 and 89, in the same order as the multiples, and it spreads the Top Breakouts scores out (item 22). The 60+ breakout line (5 Oct) needs a recheck on the new scale. Then use the agreed line wherever the score is explained.
