Four brand-deal features: first designs

For your 13 Nov roadmap. Every number in these screens is sample data.

  1. 1
    Pitch deck prep. She types a brand and gets a pitch she can paste into an email or DM, built from her own breakouts.

    Data: her TikTok and breakouts (we have them). The brand's recent TikTok videos and basic facts (new).

  2. 2
    Competitor analyzer. She pastes a creator's @ and sees that creator's breakouts from the last 30, 60 or 90 days.

    Data: the Profile Rewind reading, pointed at any public account, with a date range (mostly have).

  3. 3
    Flop video analyzer. She pastes a video that did badly and gets why, plus what to change.

    Data: the Analysis we already run, her usual views, and a new prompt for videos below her usual (mostly have).

  4. 4
    Weekly progress report. Her views, breakouts and followers for the week, counted from the week she connects TikTok.

    Data: a daily snapshot of her videos from the day she connects (new). Waits on fixing today's Profile numbers.

Brand facts for the pitch: both, BrandBeacon's brand data plus a fresh read of the brand's TikTok, as much data as we can get (your call, 13 Nov).

Each feature below: where it lives, input, processing, output, then how the output reads in a screen-recorded TikTok.
