Your note was that it looked AI-designed and too playful. We studied 19 well-made apps that show video and numbers, checked our screens against 28 published lists of AI-design tells, and rebuilt every screen to 14 rules. Same flow, same copy rules, mobile first.

The 19 reference apps

The rules we built to

  1. Corners by role: 4px for controls, 8px for cards and video, none for full-width images.
  2. No pill badges for data. Ranks, scores and multipliers are plain text.
  3. No uppercase labels above headings. A heading just says the thing.
  4. One font (Instrument Sans) with straight letter endings and real tabular numbers, three weights at most.
  5. Black for actions, one teal for breakout data, white pages. No cream, coral or gradients.
  6. Shadows only on things that float: the sheet, the banner and toasts.
  7. Lists are rows with thin dividers. No card per item.
  8. Numbers only where order is real (ranks, script steps).
  9. Motion only to show state, under 200ms. No bounce, zoom, marquee or confetti.
  10. Headlines in one color.
  11. The sales page opens with one full real breakdown.
  12. Plain copy: no 'Hi Maya', no 'Hang tight', no exclamation marks.
  13. Only true pricing claims: no crossed-out prices or 'Most popular' until they're real.
  14. The score is a number out of 100, not a gold ring.
