Three design directions, and the research behind them

We looked at 18 rival sites, 22 tools UGC creators already use, 13 creator portfolios and UB's own live brand. Each direction below follows from those findings, and every text color passes WCAG AA. Every direction also works in the full prototype: open it and use the Look switcher.

Rules all three follow

  • The thumbnails are the loudest thing on screen.Color goes on the one action and the score only. TikTok's own Explore page does the same: neutral frame, red only on Log in and the active tab.
  • Light by default. Dark follows the phone's setting.18 of 22 creator tools are light, and measured dark-mode use is 18 to 38%.
  • One action color, and it passes 4.5:1 with its text.Today's coral button fails at 3.21:1 on every screen.
  • Numbers line up and stay readable.Tabular figures, 4.5:1 for numbers, 3:1 for the score graphic (WCAG 1.4.11).
  • Pill buttons at least 48px tall, inputs at 16px or more.Pills are the most common button on creator tools; iOS zooms inputs under 16px; Apple and Android set 44pt and 48dp targets.
  • No pink, TikTok red or purple as the brand color.Pink is taken by 8 rivals, TikTok owns its red, and Stan and Canva own purple.

A · Gold

Keep what's live, fix what fails, and own the one color no rival uses.

Try it live
Why
  • Gold is used by 0 of 18 rivals, and BrandBeacon's yellow makes it a family color.
  • Keeps UB's warm paper and Plus Jakarta Sans, so the live site, X cards and video boards change the least.
  • The ink pill button scores 17.98:1, which fixes the coral button failure.
Type
Plus Jakarta Sans for everything. It's the live font, and it sits with Stan Store's in the creator-tool group.
Layout
Results become full-width tall thumbnails with the gold score on top, like the vertical feed creators scroll all day.
Trade-off
Shows 1 to 2 results per screen instead of 3.

B · Portfolio

Look like the portfolios creators make for themselves.

Try it live
Why
  • 6 of 8 creator portfolios use a serif headline and most use cream and brown, so this reads as their world, not software.
  • Only 1 of 18 rivals uses a serif, so it stands apart from the Inter-heavy field (14 of 18).
  • Sage carries the data at 5.51:1, and the espresso button scores 16.04:1.
Type
Fraunces for headlines and the hook, DM Sans for text and every number (a serif is never used for figures).
Layout
Results lead with the hook in serif, with the thumbnail small beside it, so the breakdown is what reads first.
Trade-off
Can read as lifestyle rather than a data tool; the thumbnails get smaller.

C · Sister

Make UB and BrandBeacon look like one company.

Try it live
Why
  • Uses BrandBeacon's own font (Figtree) and its yellow for the score, so a brand that knows BB trusts UB on sight.
  • Deep teal is used by 0 of 18 rivals and keeps UB's current teal, now dark enough to pass (6.70:1 with white text).
  • Clean white surfaces with crisp borders read as a paid tool, which matters at $49 a month.
Type
Figtree for everything, the same as brandbeacon.io.
Layout
Results are compact rows with bigger numbers, so you can compare more breakouts on one screen.
Trade-off
The most familiar of the three; it competes on polish, not personality.

Our pick

A · Gold

It fixes every contrast failure with the smallest change to what's live, takes the one color no rival uses, and links UB to BrandBeacon through BB's yellow. Pick B if UB should feel like the creator's own space, or C if UB and BrandBeacon should look like one product family.
